## Project Overview

Build Multi-Tenant AI Voice Receptionist for Hospitals, Real Estate & Local Shops + Appointment Bookig System| Multi-Calling AI Agents with Next.js

Build a **Multi-Tenant AI Voice Receptionist SaaS** that can serve businesses across different industries — from **hospitals and real estate companies to local shops and service businesses**.

In this project, we’ll build an AI-powered calling platform where every business can have its own **AI receptionist and multiple AI calling agents** to handle customer calls, leads, appointments, inquiries, and follow-ups.

### 🏢 Industries You Can Target

## 🏥 **Hospitals & Clinics**

Handle patient inquiries, appointment bookings, reminders, and follow-ups.

## 🏠 **Real Estate**

Capture and qualify leads, answer property questions, schedule property visits, and follow up with prospects.

## 🏪 **Local Shops & Businesses**

Answer customer calls, provide product/service information, take inquiries, and handle orders or bookings.

The same platform can also be adapted for:

- 🦷 Dental Clinics
- 💇 Salons & Spas
- 🏨 Hotels
- ⚖️ Law Firms
- 🚗 Car Dealerships
- 🔧 Home-Service Businesses
- 🍽️ Restaurants
- 🏋️ Gyms & Fitness Centers

### 🤖 AI Voice Agent Features

- Inbound AI phone calls
- Outbound AI calling
- Multiple simultaneous AI calling agents
- AI appointment booking
- Lead capture & qualification
- Customer follow-ups
- Business-specific knowledge
- Call transfers to human agents
- Call history & analytics
- Custom AI receptionist for each business

### 🏗️ Multi-Tenant SaaS

Each business gets its own isolated environment with its own:

**AI Agent → Phone Number → Business Knowledge → Customers → Leads → Appointments → Call History → Dashboard**

This makes the platform suitable for building a **B2B AI SaaS** that can onboard businesses from different industries onto one platform.
